        Standings and awards

        Update standings

        1. New event: open the Starting rank on chess-results, click inside it, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C to copy the whole page.
        2. After each round: do the same with the Crosstable.
        3. Paste the whole page here (Ctrl+V). The table is found automatically.

        Tip: you can also press Ctrl+V anywhere on the page, outside a text box.
